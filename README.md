# TG Spyglass

TG Spyglass is an unofficial, free and open-source Android app for browsing public Telegram channels. It reads public `t.me/s/<channel>` previews and stores messages locally. No Telegram account or API key is required.

TG Spyglass is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

Source code: https://github.com/Denis201-Git/TGSpyglass

## Features

- Add channels by `t.me/...` URL or username (with or without `@`)
- Local message history with versions, edits and deleted markers
- Media previews for photos, videos, documents and link previews
- Media filters for photos, videos and files
- Clickable links in message text and copy-to-clipboard
- Theme settings and English/Russian interface
- Two message styles: Classic and Borderless
- Works on 32-bit and 64-bit Android devices

## Privacy

- Channels and messages are stored locally on your device.
- The app only requests the `INTERNET` permission to read public previews and media.
- No ads, analytics, Google Play Services or tracking.

## Limitations

- Public Telegram previews do not provide comments.
- Large videos are not available in public previews; they are shown as `Media is too big` and cannot be played or downloaded.
- Direct in-app download of document files is not implemented; file cards open the related post.
